# Hey! I'm Joan Matias 👋 🇨🇺

> "Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works." — Steve Jobs

I'm a **Fullstack Developer** with 2+ years of experience, deep into **TypeScript, React, Next.js, NestJS and Python**, always looking for the best way to make things stick together. I specialize in **AI-driven development**, integrating language models into web applications, payment gateway integration, and automated testing (unit and E2E with Cypress, Jest and Vitest). I also have hands-on experience with software architecture, scalable system design, and optimized backend/frontend development (Redis caching, processing queues, WebSockets and modular architectures). I'm also deeply interested in **blockchain development** and **data science**, and I'm always exploring ways to bring those fields into my work.

- ✍️ I share my thoughts on tech at **[Medium](https://medium.com/@joanmat)**.
